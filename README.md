🧾 Billeasy – Inventory Management & Billing App

Billeasy is an Android application designed to simplify inventory management, billing, and business operations. It provides an integrated platform for managing stock, generating bills, tracking sales, and monitoring business performance through analytics.

✨ Key Features

* Inventory Management: Add, update, delete, and track inventory items.
* Stock Tracking: Monitor available stock and maintain updated inventory records.
* Bill Creator: Generate digital bills and manage customer transactions.
* Bill Scanner: Scan and capture bills using the device camera.
* Camera Integration: Capture product and bill images directly through the app.
* Sales Tracking: Maintain records of sales and transactions.
* Analytics Dashboard: Visualize important business metrics and performance.
* Revenue Monitoring: Track daily revenue and sales performance.
* Day-wise Comparison: Compare sales and revenue across different days.
* Business Insights: Understand sales trends and inventory movement through analytics.

🎯 Project Objective

The primary objective of Billeasy is to provide businesses with a simple and efficient digital solution for managing inventory and billing operations. By combining stock management, automated billing workflows, sales tracking, and analytical insights, the application aims to reduce manual effort and improve business efficiency.

🚀 Vision

To simplify everyday business management by bringing inventory, billing, and sales analytics together in one convenient application.
